Solar fine all summer, battery flat every morning in winter? Your panel probably isn't broken. ☀️

Using Bureau of Meteorology averages, we estimate the same 200W panel makes about 79Ah on a Victorian summer day but only about 22Ah in winter. That's after allowing for real-world losses.

Three things cause it: shorter days, a low sun that hits flat roof panels side-on, and long shadows across your campsite.

What helps: point a portable panel or blanket at the sun, park in the open, top up by driving, and use a bit less.
