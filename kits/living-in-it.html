The Living in it kit

Uses about 120Ah a daySized for a campervanHow we make money

Full-time or close to it. Fridge-freezer, working from the van, a diesel heater, Starlink and a morning coffee.

What it runs

  • Big 80 to 100L fridge-freezer
  • 6 LED lights
  • 2 phones, a laptop about 5 hours a day
  • Starlink Mini about 6 hours a day
  • Diesel heater electrics
  • 12V fan and water pump
  • A 1,300W coffee machine, once a day

The bits that hold it together

  • Battery box or mount, to hold it firmly in place
  • Correctly sized cables and lugs
  • Fuses or circuit breakers on every circuit
  • An isolator switch
  • Anderson plugs and a fuse block

Your auto electrician sizes these for your exact install.

Easiest way to build it

This is a full install job: separate parts, fitted and fused by an auto electrician. Ask them about a lithium-ready battery charger too, for when you plug in at a campsite.

Travelling south in winter?

Southern winters are the catch. In a Victorian winter this setup would want about 1,150W of solar, which won't fit on a van roof. Plan on a 200 to 400W portable blanket, plugging in at a powered site every few days, or a small inverter generator for grey spells.
