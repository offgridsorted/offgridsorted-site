The Road trips kit

Uses about 65 to 75Ah a dayHow we make money

A week or more away, a bigger fridge and a laptop. Some days driving, some days parked up.

What it runs

  • Large 55 to 75L fridge
  • 4 LED lights
  • 2 phones charging
  • A laptop on a 12V or USB-C charger, about 3 hours a day
  • A 12V fan overnight
  • Water pump

Make it yours

What are you powering?
What kind of solar?
Campervans: these kits are sized for you. The battery usually lives inside, so it stays cool, and the wiring run from the engine is short.
Caravans and camper trailers: fit the DC-DC charger in the van, close to the battery, fed by a heavy cable from the car through an Anderson plug. You'll get about a third less charge from driving than in a campervan because of the long cable, so lean on solar, and you'll usually have more roof space for it. A 240V battery charger for powered sites is worth adding (fitted by a licensed electrician). See Caravan power.
4WDs: In a 4WD, roof space usually fits one 200W panel at most. Pair it with a folding blanket for parked days, and a canopy is a good home for the battery. See 4WD dual battery basics.

Easiest way to build it
