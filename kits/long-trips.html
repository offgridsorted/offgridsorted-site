The Long trips kit

Uses about 65 to 75Ah a daySized for a campervanHow we make money

A week or more away, a bigger fridge and a laptop. Some days driving, some days parked up.

What it runs

  • Large 55 to 75L fridge
  • 4 LED lights
  • 2 phones charging
  • A laptop on a 12V or USB-C charger, about 3 hours a day
  • A 12V fan overnight
  • Water pump

The bits that hold it together

  • Battery box or mount, to hold it firmly in place
  • Correctly sized cables and lugs
  • Fuses or circuit breakers on every circuit
  • An isolator switch
  • Anderson plugs and a fuse block

Your auto electrician sizes these for your exact install.

Easiest way to build it

Separate parts suit this kit best, fitted by an auto electrician. Fixed roof panels are set-and-forget, and the 200Ah battery gives room to add a small inverter later.

Travelling south in winter?

In a Victorian or Tasmanian winter, plan on the bigger end of the solar range, or add a 200W blanket for long parked spells.

In a 4WD?

In a 4WD, roof space usually fits one 200W panel at most. Pair it with a folding blanket for parked days, and a canopy is a good home for the battery. See 4WD dual battery basics.
