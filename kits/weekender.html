The Weekender kit

Uses about 50Ah a daySized for a campervanHow we make money

Two or three nights away, a medium fridge, lights and phones. You drive somewhere most days.

What it runs

  • Medium 35 to 50L fridge
  • 4 LED lights, about 4 hours a night
  • 2 phones charging
  • A 12V fan overnight
  • Water pump

The bits that hold it together

  • Battery box or mount, to hold it firmly in place
  • Correctly sized cables and lugs
  • Fuses or circuit breakers on every circuit
  • An isolator switch
  • Anderson plugs and a fuse block

Your auto electrician sizes these for your exact install.

Easiest way to build it

This size is perfect for an all-in-one power box: battery, DC-DC charger and solar input in one box you can move between cars. The box itself just straps in. The heavy lead that charges it from your engine battery should be run and fused by an auto electrician.

Travelling south in winter?

Southern winters barely change this kit, because driving does most of the charging. If you often stay put in Victoria or Tasmania in winter, go for the 200W blanket.

In a 4WD?

Works well in a 4WD too. Use the solar blanket rather than roof panels, and if the battery goes under the bonnet, pick one rated for the heat. See 4WD dual battery basics.
