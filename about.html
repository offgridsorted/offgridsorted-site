About

We're Kiran and Mitch (and Ziggy, our dog). We've been living in, travelling in and troubleshooting vans for about 15 years. Off-Grid Sorted is the plain-English help we wish we'd had.

Japan, and a Delica

Kiran's love affair with van life started almost 15 years ago, on a working holiday in Japan. She converted a Mitsubishi Delica and lived in it, travelling the country from top to bottom. It started as a snow trip in the ski season and turned into a summer surf trip.

Mitch's Vito, and a lot of troubleshooting

Back in Australia, Kiran met Mitch. He had a Mercedes Vito Transformer van with a dual battery setup and a portable solar panel: our first fancy van. Lots of road trips meant lots of troubleshooting, and slowly we started to understand what was actually going on behind the fridge and the lights.

We make a good team. Mitch is the road-trip co-pilot who loves working out the building and construction side, but he's not electrical-minded. Kiran is the more electrical-minded one, but hand her a power tool at your own risk.

A slide-on camper through a Canadian COVID winter

We moved to Canada just before COVID and spent a year and a half living in a Dodge Ram 3500 with a slide-on camper, travelling all the way from Vancouver Island to Montreal. It was a godsend when everything shut down, but it came with a whole new set of power problems: much less sun, heating running inside, plus a shower, toilet and hot water.

The battery was an AGM, around 120Ah, and it was already struggling in the cold. We had a Bluetooth battery monitor fitted so we could see exactly what was going in and out. Being able to watch the numbers changed everything.

The Hiace and the beeping fridge

Back home, we bought a Toyota Hiace and did a lot of the conversion work ourselves. It's a weekender, but we wanted the fridge on all the time. It came with a Kings 115Ah AGM battery and a portable panel. We wanted the convenience of a fixed one, so we put a 160W Kings flexible panel on the roof to run our Dometic CFF45 fridge.

Then the fridge started beeping. A lot. We thought the old fridge was the problem, so we bought a new one. It kept beeping. We replaced the battery, and that worked for a while. We tested every connection. Neither of us is an electrician, and wading through all the jargon, we really struggled to work out what was wrong.

In the end we added a splitter on the solar input and plugged in a Kings solar blanket alongside the roof panel, and it started working perfectly. The roof panel simply wasn't strong enough to run the fridge the way we use it, especially when we stayed put. A simple, plain-English way to work out what we needed would have saved us a lot of time, money and heartache.

Why we built this

We live on the southern Gold Coast now, and most of our friends here and across the Northern Rivers have vans. Power is the bit that confuses almost everyone. People take very different approaches to what they need, and they get lost in the jargon. So we built the tool we wanted: tell it what you run and where you go, and it tells you what you need, in plain English.

These days you'll often find us at Delicate campground near Crescent Head. It's our dog-friendly halfway stop between Sydney and home.

How we work

  • Australian conditionsSun figures from Bureau of Meteorology averages for each state and season. Products you can buy here, in Aussie dollars.
  • Fit first, money secondBudget, mid-range and premium options, and what the extra money gets you. If the cheap one is fine, we say so.
  • Honest about limitsWe're van people, not electricians. Our numbers are estimates, and we flag when something won't fit or won't keep up in winter.
  • Safety alwaysHave a licensed auto electrician install or check your system. Never work on 240V wiring yourself.
