Privacy

The Power Planner runs in your browser. What you enter into it is not sent to us or stored by us.

On the kit pages and in the Power Planner, the "In my cart" ticks are saved only in your own browser, on your device. We can't see them.

We don't run an email list yet. If we start one, we'll only email people who sign up, every email will have an unsubscribe link, and we'll never sell your details.

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits, and affiliate partners may use cookies to know a sale came from us. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Questions about privacy? See our contact page.
