About

Off-Grid Sorted helps Australians work out the power setup for their van, caravan or 4WD, in plain English, without being sold to.

Why it exists

Ask "what battery do I need?" online and you'll mostly hear from people selling batteries. The answers are full of jargon, and they rarely start with the question that matters: what are you actually going to run, and where?

Off-Grid Sorted starts there. The Power Planner adds up what your gear uses, allows for the sun where and when you travel, and sizes a battery, solar and charger to match. The guides explain each part like a patient friend would.

How we work

  • Australian conditions. Sun figures come from Bureau of Meteorology averages for each state and season. Products are ones you can buy here, priced in Australian dollars.
  • Fit first, money second. We show budget, mid-range and premium options and say what the extra money gets you. If the cheap one is fine, we say so.
  • Honest about limits. Our numbers are estimates for planning. We flag when something won't fit on your roof or won't keep up in winter.
  • Safety always. We'll always tell you to have a licensed auto electrician install or check your system. Never work on 240V wiring yourself.

How the site is paid for

The Power Planner and guides are free. Some product links earn us a small commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. That never decides what we recommend. See the affiliate disclosure for the details.
