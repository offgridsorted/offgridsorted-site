Planning guide, not an install spec

The Power Planner gives estimates based on typical figures for appliances, sun and batteries. Real results vary with your gear, weather, heat and how you use it. Treat the results as a starting point for planning.

Get a licensed auto electrician to install or check your system. 12V and 240V systems store and move a lot of energy. Incorrect cable sizes, missing fuses or poor connections can cause damage, fire or injury. Never work on 240V wiring yourself.
