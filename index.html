Hi, we're Kiran, Mitch & Ziggy

We live on the southern Gold Coast, and the Hiace is our weekend escape. When we first set up the power, the fridge kept cutting out. We had no idea why, and the answers online were buried in jargon and sales pitches.

So we replaced the fridge. Then the battery. It took us way too long (and a fair bit of money) to work out the little roof panel just couldn't keep up when we stayed put all weekend. A solar blanket sorted it.

Turns out lots of our friends with vans were stuck on the same thing. So we've built the plain-English guide we would love to have.
